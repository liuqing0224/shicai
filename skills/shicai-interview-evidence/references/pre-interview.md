# Pre-interview contract

Input: current job profile, structured resume, reviewed report, and resume evidence.

Produce a 30-120 minute strategy with priorities and a time allocation that sums exactly to the duration. Produce 10-15 questions; the first 8 are required and the rest are optional. Cover every profile dimension and order questions by job importance, evidence uncertainty, and cost of a wrong decision.

Each question includes dimension linkage, purpose, profile requirements, resume evidence, observable strong and warning signals, estimated time, and trigger-based follow-ups. Progress from open narrative to scope, personal contribution, mechanism, production state, metrics, trade-offs, counterfactuals, and reflection.

The scorecard uses 1-4 behavioral anchors: insufficient evidence, partial, meets, and exceeds. A case exercise is optional but must specify deliverables and evaluation criteria.
